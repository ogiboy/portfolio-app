## Intent

Make the portfolio feel purposefully alive while improving cacheability and delivery discipline, and record the existing Cloudflare DNS, Email Routing, and free-edge boundaries without inventing services or overstating provider state.

## Scope

- Motion in isolated async client leaves with measurable desktop progression, mobile natural flow, and equivalent reduced-motion content.
- Static locale generation, locale-cookie removal, image/WASM lazy boundaries, and CI build-cache ownership.
- Public DNSSEC and DNS-AID index verification.
- Public contact alias, Email Routing DNS security, and user-operated forwarding evidence.
- Cloudflare/Vercel responsibility boundary and safe free-edge recommendations.
- Focused, full-gate, browser, provider, preview, and production evidence kept separate.

## Non-Goals

- Decorative perpetual motion, scroll hijacking, route-wide client rendering, or global skeletons without real blocking work.
- A Cloudflare Worker, Pages migration, outbound mailbox, broad HTML/RSC edge cache, duplicated analytics, or unmeasured response transforms.
- OAuth/OIDC, Protected Resource Metadata, `auth.md`, A2A, MCP server/card, Django, Docker, Kubernetes, Sentry, or custom analytics events.
- Guaranteed search ranking, AI citation, delivery uptime, or provider-setting completion inferred from local source.

## TDD Route

Mode: proportional verification. Unit/browser regressions and full release gates prove behavior; no strict RED/GREEN claim is made for pre-existing behavior.
