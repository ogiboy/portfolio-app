# Decision: Governance Authority and Bounded Capabilities

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Authority is ordered as follows: `AGENTS.md` operational rules; `.ai/architecture.instructions.md` current owner map; decision rationale; roadmap sequence; `.ai/checkpoints/portfolio-overhaul.md` mutable workstream state; dated Aegis initiative evidence; `package.json` commands; CI remote enforcement; `SECURITY.md` reporting; and CodeRabbit advisory guidance.

Capabilities are used progressively and do not grant external-write authority. Browser QA and orchestration each have one owner per work item. Hosted configuration follows the same evidence standard as local checks, but existing GitHub settings are changed only with explicit authority.

## Consequences

- Historical roadmap and Aegis records remain evidence, not current architecture authority.
- Agent-discovery claims require truthful source, timestamp, and uncertainty handling.
- CodeRabbit may identify issues but cannot weaken security, release, or operational rules.
